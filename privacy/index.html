Support Privacy

Privacy should be boring.

Duckmoji does not collect, store, use, or share personal data. No account. No tracking. Just duck.

Effective August 11, 2026

Data collection

Duckmoji collects no personal information or usage data. The app contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, accounts, or sign-in.

Network access

Duckmoji does not make network requests and does not send data to the developer or any third-party service.

Messages

Duckmoji runs as a sticker pack inside Apple Messages. It does not read, store, or transmit your conversations. Apple’s handling of Messages and App Store activity is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Duckmoji has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising.

Children

Because Duckmoji collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Support requests

Support is provided through public GitHub Issues. Information you choose to submit there may be publicly visible and is processed by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Do not post private messages, account details, or other personal information.

Policy changes

If Duckmoji’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Duckmoji support form.
