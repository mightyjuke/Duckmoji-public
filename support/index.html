Find Duckmoji in Messages

Duckmoji is an iMessage sticker pack. After downloading it, open a conversation in Apple Messages and follow these steps.

1

Open Stickers

Open a conversation, tap the plus button beside the message field, then choose Stickers.

2

Choose Duckmoji

Swipe through your sticker apps and tap the Duckmoji icon. If it is hidden, use Edit or More to enable it.

3

Pick a feeling

Choose from 13 original reactions, including hello, love, coffee, side-eye, party, and more.

Two ways to react

Send it

Tap a Duckmoji sticker to add it to the message field, then tap Send.

Place it

Touch and hold a sticker, then drag it onto a message, photo, or another sticker in the conversation.

Common fixes

I can’t find Duckmoji in Messages
Open the Stickers panel, find the sticker-app controls, and make sure Duckmoji is enabled. If it still does not appear, close and reopen Messages, then restart your device.
The stickers are not loading
Confirm that Duckmoji finished downloading from the App Store. Close Messages completely and reopen it. If needed, restart the device and try again.
Can I use Duckmoji on iPad?
Yes. Duckmoji supports both iPhone and iPad. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used to download the sticker pack.
Does Duckmoji require an account?
No. Duckmoji has no account, sign-in, ads, tracking, networking, or in-app purchases.

Still having a day?
