# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS for GitHub Pages, with no framework, JavaScript dependency, analytics, or tracking.

## Users

Duckmoji customers and App Review staff who need product support, installation guidance, or clear privacy information.

## Product Purpose

Provide the official public support and privacy destination for Duckmoji: Duck Having a Day, an iMessage sticker pack for iPhone and iPad.

## Positioning

Duckmoji is a small, expressive sticker pack with 13 original static duck reactions and no account, tracking, advertising, networking, purchases, or user-generated content.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from the App Store product page or while troubleshooting the sticker pack in Apple Messages. Support requests are handled through GitHub Issues in this public repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Required routes: `/support/` and `/privacy/`.
- The support page must include installation, usage, troubleshooting, and a working contact path.
- The privacy page must accurately describe a no-data-collection sticker pack.
- The public repository must not expose private source code or a personal email address.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Duckmoji name, warm yellow duck artwork, playful but trustworthy voice, and the phrase “No account. No tracking. Just duck.”

## Evidence on Hand

- App icon and original sticker artwork in the private Duckmoji project.
- Thirteen static PNG stickers for iPhone and iPad.
- Existing App Store product copy and review notes.
- No testimonials, performance claims, or customer metrics; none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make help available within seconds.
- State privacy practices plainly and specifically.
- Keep the site fast, accessible, and free of tracking.
- Let the real Duckmoji artwork provide personality without obscuring legal or support content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and readable layouts across mobile and desktop.
